ABOUT SS

BUILT BY STUDENTS

For Every Student Struggle...
Single Solution!

SS (Struggle of Student) is a student community platform built by students, for students.

WHO WE ARE

One Platform. Many Opportunities.

Our vision is to create one platform where every student gets a chance to prove themselves. SS brings together talent, skills, leadership, career opportunities and entertainment in one student-focused community.

OUR VISION

Give Every Student A Chance.

Talent

Create a platform where students can showcase their unique talents and express themselves with confidence.

Skills

Provide opportunities for students to develop communication, leadership, technical and management skills.

Leadership

Give students real responsibilities through events, teams, campus activities and leadership roles.

Career

Connect students with roles, internships, jobs and opportunities that support their career growth.

Community

Build a strong student network where students can connect, collaborate and grow together.

Entertainment

Create opportunities for students to participate, perform, enjoy and experience new activities.

WHAT WE DO

Your Talent. Your Skills. Your Opportunity.

From talent showcases and campus leadership to career roles, internships and future support, SS is designed to help students discover what they can do and give them a platform to prove it.

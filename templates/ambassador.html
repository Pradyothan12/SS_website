THE PROGRAM

One Ambassador. One Campus.

SS aims to connect students across colleges through a strong campus network. Campus Ambassadors help introduce SS to their college, connect interested students and support SS activities and initiatives.

YOUR ROLE

Lead. Connect. Represent.

Campus Representation

Represent SS and introduce the community and its opportunities to students in your college.

Student Network

Connect with students who are interested in talent, skills, events, leadership and career opportunities.

Event Support

Help coordinate and promote SS events, activities and student initiatives on your campus.

Leadership

Take responsibility, coordinate with teams and gain practical leadership and management experience.

Communication

Build confidence and improve communication by interacting with students and SS teams.

Growth

Develop real-world experience while becoming an active part of a growing student community.
